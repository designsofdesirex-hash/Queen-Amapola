# Queen Amapola Design System

## World

The site is a private-members-club / nightlife-ledger extension of Queen Amapola's public persona. It uses the established neubrutalist route: physical press feedback, hard rules, high-density type, and a limited palette with no soft creator-template chrome. The base is a dark espresso/charcoal field; bone and champagne carry the expensive feeling; rosewood is reserved for high-impact actions; gold is a hairline signal; milk blush appears only in editorial media treatment.

## Palette

- `--black` `#0B0908` — primary obsidian night surface
- `--charcoal` `#1C1714` — secondary dark field and tribute/footer surface
- `--paper` `#F2EAD9` — bone paper surface and light type
- `--red` / `--rosewood` `#6E2E2E` — reserved high-impact CTA and Alpha accent
- `--gold` `#B8924A` — antique-gold hairlines, labels, link signals, and small markers
- `--foil-gold` `#D9B872` — brighter foil highlight for the hero ring and elevated markers
- `--champagne` `#D8CBB4` — large display emphasis and soft luxury detail
- `--blush` `#E9CFC7` — limited milk-blush editorial wash, never a general UI fill

## Section Atmospheres

The page keeps the dark members-club foundation while giving each section its own muted gradient field: aubergine ink for the hero, deep green and bronze for the attention break, plum wine for tributes, rose-champagne into sage for social links, olive forest for lifestyle, misted lilac and sage for the gallery, and burgundy-plum for the close. Soft radial light is layered over each field to give depth without turning the backgrounds into bright blobs. Bone remains the text surface on dark sections; ink remains the text surface on pale sections. Rosewood stays reserved for actions.

## Typography

- Display: DM Serif Display, a calmer editorial serif with enough character for oversized headlines without the brittle hairlines of the previous Bodoni face.
- UI / metadata: Barlow Condensed, uppercase, tracked, compact, and assertive.
- Headings use aggressive scale and a `.82` line-height; red emphasis is applied through weight/color, not gradient text.

## Composition

- Full-width dark fields with generous vertical pacing; saturated color is concentrated into rosewood moments.
- Adult entry screen: 18+ confirmation with a tight image mosaic and a direct decision.
- Hero split: dominant left-aligned thesis, rotated portrait on the right.
- Tribute prompts lead to Queen Amapola's supplied Throne wishlist; her social and creator destinations live in a separate social-links section.
- The gallery shows eight images at a time from the supplied local photography and fades between sets, with previous, next, and pause controls.
- Editorial tiles, lifestyle imagery, the gallery, and tribute cards use Queen Amapola's supplied photos.
- Footer closes with the persona's own line and the five supplied outbound destinations.

## Interaction

- Primary buttons use rosewood fills; hover introduces bone/champagne contrast instead of another saturated fill.
- Buttons lift on hover and scale to `.97` on press.
- Links use short, interruptible color / transform transitions with a custom ease-out curve.
- The 18+ confirmation persists for the current browser session only; it does not verify age.
- Touch devices do not receive hover-only motion.
- Reduced-motion mode removes transform-heavy movement and preserves state changes.

## Content Rules

- Never invent services, prices, testimonials, screenshots, or private content.
- Use “tribute,” “live feed,” and the supplied persona language instead of conventional service labels.
- The adult identity should be obvious: body worship, wealth extraction, humiliation, Alpha/status, subs, and femsubs are core editorial subjects rather than hidden subtext.
- Public-language callouts must be framed as persona language or supplied examples, never as fabricated testimonials or verified customer claims.
- Keep adult-only context visible and never suggest that this page performs payment processing itself.
