# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; no backend, accounts, forms, or checkout.

## Users

Existing and prospective followers of Queen Amapola's public persona who want to follow her, browse her supplied photography, or use the supplied support links.

## Product Purpose

A focused destination for Queen Amapola's persona, editorial content, and external support links.

## Positioning

A premium web presence for a content creator, with social channels as the live destination and support links as deliberate actions. This is a Designs of Desire studio project and must not read as a generic SaaS or corporate template.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from social media or direct sharing, confirm they are 18+ for the current browser session, scan the editorial page and gallery, and follow Amapola's X, YouPay, Throne, or Telegram links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Fully static; no backend, accounts, forms, checkout, or custom-service booking flow. Adult confirmation is session-only and does not verify age. The page preserves the existing text, titles, subtitles, and adult copy; only the creator name, links, and images are adapted. The YouTube section is removed. Outbound destinations supplied by the user are [X](https://x.com/diosa_amapola), [YouPay](https://youpay.me/goddessamapola_), [YouPay bio](https://youpay.me/goddessamapola_/bio), [Throne](https://throne.com/goddessamapola_), and [Telegram](https://t.me/amapola44).

## Brand Commitments

The creator identity is Queen Amapola, a Latina creator. The adapted page keeps the source's dominant, status-focused, bratty, sexually suggestive, adult-only voice and its existing content structure except for the removed YouTube section.

## Evidence on Hand

The project contains 18 user-supplied photos, locally optimized as WebP in `assets/photos/amapola/`, used across the age gate, landing section, lifestyle section, tributes, footer, and gallery. All five external URLs were supplied by the user. No YouTube channel or YouTube media is supplied.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the established visual system and all existing page text while using Queen Amapola's supplied photos and destination links.
- Keep support actions clear and link visitors directly to their external destinations.
- Do not invent services, prices, social proof, or private content.
- Keep adult-only context visible and do not suggest the page processes payments.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect adult-only framing, maintain readable contrast, provide visible keyboard focus, preserve semantic link labels, and support reduced motion.
